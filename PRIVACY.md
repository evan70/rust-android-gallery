# Privacy

Android Rust Gallery collects nothing and sends nothing. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and
no network access. Your data stays on your phone, in files you can read yourself.

Permissions, and what each is for:

- Read photos and videos, to show them.
- All files access, to move, hide and trash media.
- Show over other apps and notifications, for the floating video player.
- Fingerprint or screen lock, for the hidden album.

If this ever changes it will be written here first.
